A short summary of me

Past

Retail customer team member through to assistant manager, Self employed painter decorator, extended to sub-contractor across many disciplines. I started learning to program in 2023, at hobby pace, on Codecademy. A friend later introduced me to iO Academy, in 2023 I attended the in person taster session that was apart of Bath Digital Festival. Using HTML and CSS I was guided through building a maze for the users cursor, using already written JavaScript. After I continued with Codecademy until I could break from contracted work and pursue the change of career. In August 2024 I entered iO Academy again but as a student on their Full Stack Track 16 weeks course. My cohort of 8 finished in December having learnt several languages, frameworks and tools.

Present

At present I am building projects, extending this portfolio, and reinforcing my education whilst seeking opportunities. I have still been working self-employed to support myself.

Future

I will work towards being apart of a collaborative team giving meaningful contribution. I look to be somewhere I can grow within a team and business, with support and determination to always improve.
