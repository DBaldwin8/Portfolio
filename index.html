Past

In July 2023, after discussing programming with a friend, I attended a taster session at iO Academy, where I built a maze game using HTML and CSS. I then started Codecademy around my work until I could transition careers. In August 2024, I joined iO Academy’s Full Stack Track 16-week course. My cohort of eight completed the course in December. The intensive training equipped me with valuable skills learning several languages and frameworks.

Present

At present I am building projects, extending this portfolio, and reinforcing my education whilst seeking opportunities. I have still been working self-employed to support myself.

Future

I will work towards being apart of a collaborative team giving meaningful contribution. I look to be somewhere I can grow within a team and business, with support and determination to always improve.
